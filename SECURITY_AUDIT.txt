Recommended legitimate solution when a code-signing certificate is required:
- Obtain a valid Authenticode code-signing certificate from an approved certificate authority.
- Sign the final EXE and, where applicable, the bundled runtime or installer with that certificate.
- Validate the signature on a clean Windows system before publication.
- Continue to avoid deceptive packaging, obfuscation, loaders, or bypass techniques.
